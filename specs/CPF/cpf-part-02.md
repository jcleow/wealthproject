# CPF Playbook Part 2: Understanding Retirement Needs

## The 3 Core Needs for Secure Retirement

| Need | Description | Key Points |
|------|-------------|------------|
| **1. Fully Paid Home** | Housing security | Pay off mortgage before retirement; can monetize (rent, Lease Buyback) |
| **2. Medical Safety Net** | Healthcare coverage | Hospital & Surgical Plan + Long-term Care Insurance |
| **3. Reliable Income Stream** | Lifelong income | CPF LIFE recommended for high payout certainty |

## The 5 Risks Retirees Face

| Risk | Description | Mitigation |
|------|-------------|------------|
| **1. Inflation** | ~2% annually erodes purchasing power | Ensure savings earn above inflation |
| **2. Longevity** | Outliving savings | Extend working career; each year deferred = ~2 extra years savings |
| **3. Healthcare** | ~12% medical inflation; 1 in 2 age 65+ may become disabled | Hospital plan + long-term care insurance |
| **4. Investment** | Sequence of returns risk | Don't rely solely on investments for essentials |
| **5. Overspending** | Depleting savings too fast | Categorize essential vs discretionary spending |

## Sequence of Returns Risk (Critical Concept)

- **Accumulators** (no withdrawals): Sequence doesn't matter
- **Retirees** (with withdrawals): Early losses can deplete portfolio completely
- Example: Portfolio A (early losses) ran out at age 81; Portfolio B (early gains) had $2.45M at age 90
