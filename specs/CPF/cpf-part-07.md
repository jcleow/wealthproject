# CPF Playbook Part 7: CPF LIFE Plans & Estate Planning

## Escalating Plan Details

- Starting payout ~20% lower than Standard
- **Increases 2% annually** for inflation protection
- Eventually exceeds both Standard and Basic

**Break-even Points**:

| vs Plan | Monthly Payout | Cumulative |
|---------|----------------|------------|
| Standard | ~12 years (age 77) | ~23 years (age 88) |
| Basic | ~8 years (age 73) | ~15 years (age 80) |

## CPF LIFE Plans Comparison (FRS at 65)

## Estate Planning: CPF Nomination

**Critical**: CPF savings **cannot be distributed via a will**. Must use CPF Nomination.

### Three Nomination Types

| Type | Method | Details |
|------|--------|---------|
| **Cash** | Distribute in cash | Default option |
| **ENS** | Transfer to beneficiaries' CPF | Two options (SA/RA first or MA first) |
| **SNSS** | Monthly disbursement | For special needs child |

### ENS Options

**Option 1** (SA/RA Priority): SA/RA first → MA → Cash
**Option 2** (MA Priority): MA first → SA/RA → Cash

### Nomination Rules

- Requires **2 witnesses** (in person or CPF Service Centre)
- Cash Nomination can be done **online**
- ENS/SNSS must be **in person**
- **Marriage revokes** existing nomination
- **Divorce**: Nomination remains valid

**URLs**:
- Make: https://www.cpf.gov.sg/makecpfnom
- View: https://www.cpf.gov.sg/viewnomdetails

## Tips for Your Children

| Method | Account | Tax Relief | Benefit |
|--------|---------|------------|---------|
| SA via RSTU | SA | **No** | Larger CPF LIFE payouts (locked until 55-65) |
| MA Top-up | MA | **No** | Healthcare safety net |
| VC3A | OA/SA/MA | **No** | Boosts housing savings |

**Example**: $200/month to newborn's SA for 30 years:
- Total contributed: $72,000
- SA at age 30: **$157,000** (vs $77,000 in bank)

## Tips for Working Adults (Under 55)

**Strategy A: OA → SA Transfer**
- Move from 2.5% to 4%
- Up to 5% effective on first $60k

**Strategy B: CPF Top-Ups**

| Scheme | Account | Tax Relief |
|--------|---------|------------|
| MA Top-Up | MediSave | Up to $8,000 |
| RSTU | SA | Up to $8,000 |
| VC3A | OA/SA/MA | None (employees) |

**RSTU "While You Can"**: Once SA reaches FRS, no more top-up room available
