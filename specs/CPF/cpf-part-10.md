## The Six "Threes" of CPF

### 3 Key Objectives
1. Fully paid-up home
2. Insurance/savings for healthcare
3. Lifelong retirement income

### 3 CPF Accounts
1. OA - housing
2. SA/RA - retirement
3. MA - healthcare

### 3 Contributors
1. Employee (20%)
2. Employer (17%)
3. Government (interest up to 6%)

### 3 Retirement Sums
1. BRS - basic, excludes rent
2. FRS - includes rent (recommended)
3. ERS - maximum payouts

### 3 CPF LIFE Plans
1. Basic - highest bequest
2. Standard - highest payout
3. Escalating - inflation protection

### 3 Nomination Schemes
1. Cash - in cash
2. ENS - to beneficiaries' CPF
3. SNSS - monthly for special needs

## 1M65 Strategy

**Goal**: $1 million in CPF savings by age 65 (as a couple)

### 4 Requirements
1. Each partner achieves **$500k** in CPF
2. Cash top-ups to reach **$130k in SA+MA by early 30s**
3. Monthly salary at least **$6,000** till retirement
4. Don't overspend on property

## Quick Calculation: CPF LIFE Payout

**Formula**: RA at 55 ÷ divisor = monthly payout at 65

| Gender | Divisor | Example ($213k) |
|--------|---------|-----------------|
| Male | 120 | $1,775/month |
| Female | 132 | $1,614/month |

**Adjustments**:
- Basic: 90% of Standard
- Escalating: 80% of Standard (grows 2%/year)
- Deferment: +7% per year
