# CPF Quick Reference (2025)

> **For LLMs**: Use this for quick lookups of common CPF values.

## Retirement Sums

| Sum | Amount | Purpose |
|-----|--------|---------|
| **BRS** | $106,500 | Basic living, excludes rent |
| **FRS** | $213,000 | Includes rent (recommended) |
| **ERS** | $426,000 | Maximum payouts |

## Basic Healthcare Sum

**BHS (2025)**: $75,500

## CPF LIFE Payouts at Age 65

| Sum | Standard Plan |
|-----|---------------|
| BRS | $860 - $930 |
| FRS | $1,610 - $1,730 |
| ERS | $3,100 - $3,300 |

## CPF LIFE Plans

| Plan | Starting Payout | Bequest | Best For |
|------|-----------------|---------|----------|
| Standard | Highest | Lowest | Max income |
| Basic | ~10% lower | Highest | Inheritance |
| Escalating | ~20% lower | Moderate | Inflation |

## Interest Rates

| Account | Base | With Additional |
|---------|------|-----------------|
| OA | 2.5% | 3.5% (first $20k below 55), 4.5% (55+) |
| SA/MA/RA | 4.0% | 5% (first $60k below 55), 6% (55+) |

## Contribution Rates (Age ≤55, 2025)

- Employee: 20%
- Employer: 17%
- **Total: 37%**
- Annual Limit: $37,740
- OW Ceiling: $7,400/month

## Tax Relief Limits

| Scheme | Self | Loved Ones |
|--------|------|------------|
| RSTU | $8,000 | $8,000 |
| MA Top-up | Shared with RSTU | $8,000 |

## CPF LIFE Deferment

**+7% per year** (ages 65-70)

| Start Age | Increase |
|-----------|----------|
| 66 | +7% |
| 67 | +14% |
| 68 | +23% |
| 70 | +40% |

## Quick Payout Calculation

**RA at 55 ÷ 120 (male) or 132 (female) = monthly payout at 65**
