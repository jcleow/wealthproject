# CPF Playbook Part 5: Wealth Accumulation Strategies 1-6

## 10 Strategies Overview

| # | Strategy | For | Key Benefit |
|---|----------|-----|-------------|
| 1 | Regular Work Contributions | Employees | Mandatory + employer contribution |
| 2 | Self-Employed Contributions | Self-Employed | MediSave (mandatory) |
| 3 | VC3A | All | Build all 3 accounts |
| 4 | MediSave Top-Up | All | Healthcare + tax relief |
| 5 | RSTU | All | SA/RA top-up + tax relief |
| 6 | CPF Transfers | All | Higher interest (OA→SA/RA) |

---

## Strategy 1: Regular Work Contributions

**Wage Ceilings (2025)**:
- Ordinary Wage: $7,400/month (increases to $8,000 from Jan 2026)
- Annual Wage: $102,000

**Contribution Rates (Jan 2025)**:

| Age | Employee | Employer | Total |
|-----|----------|----------|-------|
| ≤55 | 20% | 17% | **37%** |
| 55-60 | 17% | 15.5% | 32.5% |
| 60-65 | 11.5% | 12% | 23.5% |
| 65-70 | 7.5% | 9% | 16.5% |
| 70+ | 5% | 7.5% | 12.5% |

**CPF Annual Limit**: $37,740

---

## Strategy 2: Self-Employed Contributions

- Only **MediSave mandatory** (if NTI > $6,000)
- Must continue even after BHS (excess → SA/RA)
- Tax relief: Lower of 37% of NTI, $37,740, or actual amount

---

## Strategy 3: Voluntary Contributions to All 3 Accounts (VC3A)

- Top up all 3 accounts up to $37,740 annual limit
- Allocation follows age band rates
- **Key**: SA allocation still goes to SA even after reaching FRS
- Self-employed tax relief available

---

## Strategy 4: MediSave Top-Up Only

- Top up MA up to prevailing BHS
- **Tax relief**: Up to $8,000/year (shared with RSTU)
- Additional $8,000 for loved ones' MA
- **Total potential**: $16,000/year tax relief

---

## Strategy 5: Retirement Sum Topping-Up Scheme (RSTU)

| Age | Top-Up To | Cap |
|-----|-----------|-----|
| Below 55 | Special Account | Prevailing FRS |
| 55+ | Retirement Account | Prevailing ERS |

**Tax Relief**: $8,000 self + $8,000 loved ones

**4 Advantages of Reaching FRS Early**:
1. Can transfer CPF to family members
2. Use OA for multiple properties without limits
3. Greater liquidity from age 55
4. Compound interest works for you

---

## Strategy 6: CPF Transfers

| Transfer | Cap | When |
|----------|-----|------|
| OA → SA | Prevailing FRS | Below 55 |
| OA → RA | Prevailing ERS | 55+ |

**Trade-offs** (transfers are irreversible):
- Cannot use for housing
- Reduces RSTU room
- No tax relief
- No MRSS matching

**Recommendation**: Use RSTU cash top-ups first (tax relief), then transfers
