# CPF Playbook Part 4: Interest Rates, BHS & Retirement Sums

## CPF Interest Rates

| Account | Base Rate | Floor |
|---------|-----------|-------|
| **OA** | 3-month average of 3 local banks | 2.5% p.a. |
| **SA/MA/RA** | 12-month yield of 10-year SGS + 1% | 4% p.a. |

## Additional Interest (Below Age 55)

| Condition | Extra Interest | Cap |
|-----------|---------------|-----|
| First $60,000 combined | +1% p.a. | $20,000 from OA |

**Effective rates**: OA (first $20k): 3.5% | SA/MA: 5%

## Additional Interest (Age 55+)

| Tier | Balance | Extra Interest |
|------|---------|---------------|
| First $30,000 | +2% p.a. | $20k from OA |
| Next $30,000 | +1% p.a. | $20k from OA |

**Effective rates**: OA (first $20k): 4.5% | RA/MA: up to 6%

## Basic Healthcare Sum (BHS)

**2025 BHS**: $75,500 (for those turning 65 in 2025)

| Age | BHS Behavior |
|-----|--------------|
| Below 65 | Adjusted annually |
| 65+ | Fixed for life |

**MA Overflow**: When MA reaches BHS, excess → SA (until FRS) → OA

## Retirement Sums (2025)

| Sum | Amount | Purpose |
|-----|--------|---------|
| **BRS** | $106,500 | Basic living, **excludes rent** |
| **FRS** | $213,000 | Higher payouts, **includes rent** (default) |
| **ERS** | $426,000 | Maximum payouts |

## CPF LIFE Payout Estimates at Age 65

| Sum | Monthly Payout |
|-----|----------------|
| BRS | $860 - $930 |
| FRS | $1,610 - $1,730 |
| ERS | $3,100 - $3,300 |

## Property Pledge for BRS

- Requires property with lease to age 95
- Can cover up to **half** of FRS
- Only **cash component** used for CPF LIFE calculation

## Why Retirement Sums Increase

1. **Inflation**: ~2% long-term
2. **Longevity**: Life expectancy at 65 increased from 18.0 years (2003) to 21.2 years (2024)
3. **Living Standards**: Higher income → higher expectations
