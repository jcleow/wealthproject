## SA vs MA: Which to Top Up First?

| Factor | SA | MA |
|--------|-----|-----|
| Limit | FRS ($213,000) | BHS ($75,500) |
| After limit | Stays in SA | Overflows to SA → OA |
| Tax relief | Direct | Direct |

**MA First**: Offsets insurance premiums; overflow builds retirement automatically
**SA First**: "While you can" scheme - no room once FRS reached

## Buying Your House

**CPF Housing Scheme** allows OA for: Downpayment, Mortgage, Stamp/legal fees, HPS premiums

**4 Questions Before Cash vs OA**:
1. Sufficient cash flow?
2. Can cash earn ≥2.5% consistently?
3. Plan to sell property later?
4. Have emergency fund?

**Mortgage Example** (Kate, age 30-54, $300/month for 25 years):

| Scenario | Total at 54 | Change |
|----------|-------------|--------|
| 100% from OA | $173,000 | -14% |
| 50/50 split | $187,000 | -6% |
| 100% from bank | $201,000 | **+0.9%** |

## Selling Your House

**Must refund to CPF**:
1. Housing grants + accrued interest
2. CPF principal withdrawn
3. Accrued interest

| Age | Refund Goes To |
|-----|----------------|
| Below 55 | OA (can transfer to SA) |
| 55+ | RA first (up to FRS), then OA |

**Strategy**: Voluntary refund before selling reduces accrued interest owed

## OA + SA Shielding Strategy

**Goal**: Deposit idle cash into CPF while maintaining liquidity after 55

### The "Hack"

**Step 1 (Before 55)**: Use CPFIS to invest amounts above minimums
- OA: Keep $20,000, invest rest
- SA: Keep $40,000, invest rest
- Use low-risk instruments (T-bills, MMFs)

**Step 2 (At 55)**: RA created
- Combined $60k < FRS → All goes to RA
- Top up RA with **cash** to FRS ($213k - $60k = $153k)

**Step 3 (After 55)**: Liquidate investments
- SA closed → Proceeds go to OA
- FRS met → **OA fully withdrawable**

### Example: Yusof

| Before Shielding | After Shielding |
|------------------|-----------------|
| OA: $258k, SA: $247k | OA: $20k, SA: $40k, Invested: $445k |

**Result**: $445k returns to OA (liquid!) + RA earning 4% p.a.
