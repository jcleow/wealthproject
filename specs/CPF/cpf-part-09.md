# CPF Playbook Part 9: At 55, ERS Decision & CPF LIFE Alternatives

## Pre-Retirees at 55: What Happens

### RA Creation Process
1. SA → RA (up to FRS)
2. If SA < FRS: OA → RA (until FRS met)
3. SA closes at 55

### OA Withdrawal Rules
- Can withdraw once RA meets FRS (or BRS with property pledge)
- Withdrawable: OA balance only
- **Not withdrawable**: Interest earned, government grants, top-ups

### Opportunity Cost Example (Kate at 55)

| Path | Action | CPF LIFE at 65 | Total at 65 |
|------|--------|----------------|-------------|
| A: Withdraw OA | Take $150k, invest at 4% | $1,730/month | $372k (invested) + CPF LIFE |
| B: Keep in OA | Leave $150k (2.5%) | $1,730/month | $192k (OA) + CPF LIFE |
| C: Transfer OA→RA | Move $150k to RA | **$2,820/month** | CPF LIFE only |

## Should You Top Up to ERS?

**ERS (2025)**: $426,000 (4x BRS)

### Decision Framework

| Scenario | Action |
|----------|--------|
| RA ≥ ERS | No action needed |
| FRS ≤ RA < ERS | Consider: Need higher guaranteed income? |
| RA < FRS | Priority: Reach FRS first |

### Considerations

| Factor | Top Up to ERS | Don't Top Up |
|--------|---------------|--------------|
| Risk appetite | Low | Higher |
| Income certainty | Want guaranteed | Comfortable with investments |
| Bequest priority | Less concerned | Want to leave more |
| Liquidity | Don't need funds | May need access |

## CPF LIFE Plan Selection (3 Questions)

2. **Want highest initial payout?** → Standard Plan
3. **Bequest important?** → Basic Plan

## When to Start CPF LIFE Payouts

**Window**: Age 65-70
**Deferment benefit**: **7% increase per year**

| Start Age | Payout (FRS) | Break-even |
|-----------|--------------|------------|
| 65 | $1,730 | - |
| 66 | $1,850 (+7%) | Age 76 |
| 67 | $1,980 (+14%) | Age 77 |
| 68 | $2,120 (+23%) | Age 78 |
| 70 | $2,430 (+40%) | Age 80 |

**Auto-start**: Age 70 if not initiated

## Alternatives to CPF LIFE

### Option 1: Fixed Deposits
- **Pros**: Capital guaranteed, flexible, SDIC insured
- **Cons**: Low rates (~3%), not lifelong

### Option 2: Higher-Risk Investments
- **Pros**: Higher potential returns, dividend income
- **Cons**: Volatility, sequence of returns risk

### Option 3: Insurance Annuity
- **Pros**: Lifelong income, customizable features
- **Cons**: Lower payout per dollar vs CPF LIFE

---

## CPF LIFE vs Private Annuity

| Provider | Payout per $1 Premium |
|----------|----------------------|
| **CPF LIFE** | **$0.10** |
| Private insurers | $0.06 - $0.07 |

**Why CPF LIFE pays more**:
1. Government-backed (no insolvency premium)
2. Risk pooling across population
3. Lower admin costs
4. Non-profit
