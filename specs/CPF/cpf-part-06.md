# CPF Playbook Part 6: Strategies 7-10 & CPF LIFE Introduction

## Strategy 7: Voluntary Housing Refund

**Purpose**: Refund CPF used for mortgage back to OA

**Benefits**:
- Reduces CPF principal + accrued interest owed
- More cash when selling property
- Earns 2.5% p.a. in OA
- **Only strategy to credit money directly into OA**

**After 55**: Refund goes to RA first (up to FRS), excess stays in OA

---

## Strategy 8: CPF Investment Scheme (CPFIS)

**Eligibility**: 18+, not bankrupt, complete SAQ

**Investment Restrictions**:

| Account | Cannot Invest | Can Invest |
|---------|---------------|------------|
| OA | First $20,000 | Remainder |
| SA | First $40,000 | Remainder |

**Limits (from OA)**:
- Up to 35%: Shares, REITs, Corporate Bonds
- Up to 10%: Gold ETFs

**Tax**: Gains, interest, dividends are **non-taxable**

**Havend Recommendation**: Discourage SA investments - hard to beat risk-free 5%

---

## Strategy 9: Matched Retirement Savings Scheme (MRSS)

**Government matches** cash top-ups to RA:
- Up to $2,000/year
- Lifetime limit: $20,000

**Eligibility**:
- Singapore Citizen, 55+
- RA < cohort's BRS
- Avg monthly income ≤ $4,000
- Property AV ≤ $21,000
- Own ≤ 1 property

---

## Strategy 10: Matched MediSave Scheme (MMSS)

**New scheme (2026-2030)**: Government matches MA top-ups up to $1,000/year

**Eligibility**: Age 55-70, MA < half of BHS, similar income/property limits

---

## CPF LIFE Introduction

**CPF LIFE**: CPF Lifelong Income For the Elderly

**How it works**:
- Pools interest earned on premiums of all members
- Provides **lifelong payouts** even after exhausting own premium
- RA/CPF LIFE earns **4-6% p.a.** risk-free

**Auto-Inclusion** (if born 1958+):
- Singapore Citizen or PR
- ≥ $60,000 in RA when payouts start

**Payout Timing**:
- Eligibility: Age 65-70
- Defer for **7% increase per year**
- Auto-starts at 70 if not initiated

## Three CPF LIFE Plans

| Plan | Starting Payout | Bequest | Premium Used |
|------|-----------------|---------|--------------|
| **Standard** (default) | Highest | Lowest | 100% of RA |
| **Basic** | ~10% lower | Higher | 10-20% of RA |
| **Escalating** | ~20% lower | Moderate | 100% of RA |
