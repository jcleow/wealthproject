# CPF Playbook Part 3: 3Cs Framework & CPF Basics

## The 3Cs of Financial Planning

| C | Focus | Key Guidelines |
|---|-------|----------------|
| **Cash Flow** | Spending & Saving | <40% gross income on loans; Save ≥15%; Emergency fund = 3-6 months |
| **Coverage** | Insurance | Get coverage needed, pay as little as possible; Don't mix with savings |
| **CPF** | Retirement | Optimize during working years; Use RSTU; Goal = reach FRS ASAP |

## 5 Pillars of Insurance Coverage

1. Hospital and Surgical
2. Critical Illness
3. Life Insurance
4. Disability Income
5. Long-Term Care

**In Retirement, Keep Only**: Hospital & Surgical + Long-Term Care

## CPF Background

- **1955**: Introduced - 5% employee + 5% employer
- **2009**: CPF LIFE introduced
- **2025**: CPF turns 70 years old

## CPF Account Types

| Account | Purpose | Interest Rate |
|---------|---------|---------------|
| **OA** (Ordinary) | Housing, education, retirement | ≥2.5% p.a. |
| **SA** (Special) | Retirement savings, investment | ≥4% p.a. |
| **MA** (MediSave) | Medical expenses, insurance premiums | ≥4% p.a. |
| **RA** (Retirement) | Created at 55, provides retirement payouts | ≥4% p.a. |

## What Happens at Age 55

**Scenario 1: SA > FRS**
- FRS amount from SA → RA
- Remaining SA → OA (withdrawable)
- SA closes

**Scenario 2: SA < FRS**
- Entire SA → RA
- OA → RA until FRS met
- Remaining OA withdrawable

**Scenario 3: SA + OA < FRS**
- First $5,000 stays in OA (withdrawable)
- Everything else → RA
- Not required to top up to FRS
